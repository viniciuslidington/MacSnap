# MacSnap

MacSnap organizes windows into positions on a display and lets the user adjust the split between an eligible pair.

## Language

**Window**: One independently positioned window belonging to an application. Several windows may belong to the same application.

**Snap Zone**: A named geometric position in a display's usable area, such as a half, third, quarter, or maximized area.

**Snap Intent**: The user's choice of a snap zone and whether it represents an exact layout slot or an adaptive fit beside an existing window.

**Pair**: Two distinct windows that share an eligible split on one display, side by side or stacked.

**Split Ratio**: The proportion of the usable area assigned to the first window of a pair: left when side by side, top when stacked.

**Resize Operation**: One adjustment of a pair's split, from the initial interaction through the resulting arrangement of its windows.

**Drag Gesture**: One continuous attempt to move a window, ending when the mouse button is released or the interaction is cancelled.

**Window Observation**: The identity, geometry, and visibility information available about a window at an observation time.

**Occlusion**: Another window covering a point at which the paired divider would otherwise be interactive.
